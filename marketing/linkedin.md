**Post:**
For the past few months, I’ve been working on a problem that almost everyone faces: "Decision Fatigue" in front of the closet. 

Today, I’m incredibly proud to announce the launch of **VoiceDress** — the world's first voice-powered AI stylist and virtual fitting room. 

I realized that traditional wardrobe apps were failing because they forced users to manually scroll and assemble outfits on a tiny screen. It felt like work. So, I completely removed the interface. 

With VoiceDress, you simply upload a photo of yourself and your clothes (or screenshots of items you want to buy). Then, you just speak.

🗣️ *"I need a smart-casual outfit for an investor meeting."*
🗣️ *"Swap the shoes for sneakers."*

The AI checks the live weather forecast, curates the perfect outfit from your closet, and uses advanced image generation to style those exact clothes onto your photo. You see exactly how you'll look before you even open your closet doors.

I’d love for my network to try it out and share feedback!
