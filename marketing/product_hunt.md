# Product Hunt Launch

*Product Hunt is the best place to get initial users and tech press. Post between 12:01 AM and 1:00 AM PST on a Tuesday or Wednesday for maximum visibility.*

**Name:** VoiceDress
**Tagline (max 60 chars):** Dress without deciding. Your AI voice stylist.
**Description (max 260 chars):** 
VoiceDress is a virtual fitting room powered by your voice. Upload your clothes (or screenshots of items you want to buy), tell the AI where you're going, and instantly see yourself styled for the occasion. Stop scrolling, start speaking.

**Maker's Comment (Post this immediately after launching):**
> Hi Product Hunt! 👋 I'm Bassey, the creator of VoiceDress.
> 
> I built VoiceDress because I was tired of staring at my closet for 20 minutes every morning trying to figure out what to wear. Traditional wardrobe apps make you scroll through endless grids of clothes, which takes just as much time!
> 
> VoiceDress changes that by introducing Voice UI to fashion. 
> 🎙️ **How it works:**
> 1. Upload a full-body photo of yourself.
> 2. Upload photos of your clothes (or screenshots from Zara/SHEIN).
> 3. Tap the mic and say: *"I need an outfit for a dinner date"* or *"Swap the shoes for something more casual."*
> 
> The AI instantly styles the outfit directly onto your photo, factoring in the live weather forecast. It’s like having a personal stylist who actually knows what’s in your closet.
> 
> I’d love to hear your feedback, and I'll be here all day answering questions! 🚀
